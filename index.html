Projects

Human-in-the-loop AI governance is easy to write into a policy document and hard to enforce once a system is running. These five projects are that enforcement, built and published as working software: a rule that a model may propose a value but only a named human can attest it, applied to real operational references where wrong guidance has consequences.

Curriculum · Live

Singapore Math — Grade 6

A full-year, 149-day Grade 6 math curriculum built on the Singapore Math CPA (Concrete–Pictorial–Abstract) method, across 10 units with seven purpose-built interactive manipulatives — bar models, a balance scale for algebra, a double number line for ratios, an angle-chase tool for geometry, and more. Every lesson names its own accessibility channel and mastery criteria rather than relying on a boilerplate note.

Curriculum Designer & Developer: David Petry, developed in collaboration with Claude (Anthropic)

Curriculum · Live

Human-Centered AI Literacy

A twelve-lesson AI literacy course for educators, professionals and students: how a model works, how to check what it says, how to judge what it builds, and how to recognize manipulation, from people and from AI. Short Essentials cover staying secure: scams, cloned voices, AI phone bots and hacked accounts. Every lesson follows the CPA method, with a real AI output learners mark, check or rewrite, and every factual claim is an attestation-ledger record that shows whether a named practitioner has attested it or it is still a model's proposal.

Curriculum Designer & Developer: David Petry, developed in collaboration with Claude (Anthropic)

npm package · MIT

attestation-ledger

A provenance engine for expert assertions. Every value carries who asserted it, what grounds it, and when — and status is derived from that verification date, so an attestation decays on its own unless somebody re-checks it. No combination of fields can promote a model-generated value to attested; there is no code path from machine output to human sign-off.

JavaScript · zero dependencies · 21 tests · CI
npm Source
Web app · Live

Live Sound EQ SOP

A field reference for front-of-house audio engineers — channel EQ starting points and aux routing for an Allen & Heath MixWizard WZ3. Every value is either attested by a named practitioner or clearly flagged as an unverified AI proposal, never blended together as fact. Console specifications validated against the manufacturer's manual.

Web app · Live

DJ Mixing SOP

Procedures for running a DJ source through a two-sub, two-main FOH rig — patching, gain staging, matching mismatched subwoofers by ear, and troubleshooting. Fifty-four attested records across two operating modes, with a genre overlay that layers onto the core procedure rather than duplicating it.

JavaScript · 18 tests · GitHub Actions · Netlify
Live site Source
Why this matters beyond audio. The engine knows nothing about sound — it was extracted from the first project, published to npm, and is now consumed as a real dependency by both audio applications and the AI literacy course. The same rule that governs a kick drum EQ value governs a monitor routing policy, and would govern a clinical protocol, a safety limit, or an editorial standard. As models generate more operational content, the difference between a practitioner verified this and a model produced something plausible becomes load-bearing. This makes that difference a data structure rather than a hope.

Philosophy & Practice

Human-Centered AI

AI extends human capability — it doesn't replace judgment, accountability, or relationships. Skepticism about AI is often well-founded, and part of my work is knowing when not to use it.

What AI Is

  • A pattern-prediction tool — a force multiplier whose output quality depends on the person directing it.

What AI Is Not

  • Not sentient, not accountable, not neutral — and not a substitute for expertise or trust.

Ethics

  • Disclose AI use, respect attribution and consent, check for bias — the human owns the result.

Governance

  • Human-in-the-loop for consequential decisions, documented workflows, written policy before rollout.
